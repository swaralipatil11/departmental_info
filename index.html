About the Department

Vision

To be a center of excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, nurturing innovative, ethical, and industry-ready engineers capable of solving real-world problems through data-driven intelligence.

Mission

To impart quality education in AI and Data Science through strong fundamentals, hands-on practice, and research; to foster innovation and entrepreneurship; and to inculcate ethical, lifelong learning habits among students.

History

Established in 2020 to meet the fast-growing demand for AI and Data Science professionals, the department has quickly grown into one of the most sought-after programs in the institute, backed by modern labs and an industry-aligned curriculum.

Highlights

  • GPU-enabled AI/ML and Data Science laboratories
  • Industry collaborations, internships, and live project opportunities
  • Active AI & DS student club organizing hackathons and workshops
  • Dedicated research cell for publications and patent filing
